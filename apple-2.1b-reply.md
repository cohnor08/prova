Hello,

Prova is completely free. There is no In-App Purchase, no subscription, no external purchase path, and no paid tier of any kind in this build.

1. WHO USES THE PAID SERVICES?
Nobody. There are no paid services. All three account types (Student, Personal, Teacher) get every feature free, with no limits and no paid tier available to them.

2. WHERE CAN USERS PURCHASE SUBSCRIPTIONS?
Nowhere. There are no subscriptions to buy, inside the app or outside it. There is no website checkout, no payment processor, and no "contact us to upgrade" route. The app contains no prices, no plan selector, no upgrade button and no paywall. The binary includes no billing SDK — no StoreKit purchase code, no RevenueCat, no Stripe.

3. WHAT PREVIOUSLY PURCHASED CONTENT CAN BE ACCESSED?
None. Prova has never sold anything, so no user has a prior purchase to access or restore. There is deliberately no Restore Purchases flow. Every account is fully unlocked from sign-up, with no entitlement check, licence key or promo code.

4. WHAT PAID FEATURES ARE UNLOCKED WITHOUT IN-APP PURCHASE?
None. No feature sits behind a payment of any kind — practice plans, progress tracking, the song library, all mini-games, messaging, the AI assistant and the full teacher toolkit are free for everyone.

ONE FEATURE WE WANT TO FLAG PROACTIVELY
Prova has a Performance Mode for musicians playing live. It displays a QR code that the audience at the venue can scan to open a web page and request a song. If the performer has optionally saved their own payment link (their own PayPal, Venmo or Cash App), that web page also shows it so an audience member can tip them.

We believe this sits outside In-App Purchase because it is a person-to-person payment for a real-world live performance, not digital content. Nothing in the app is unlocked by it. Prova does not process, hold or take any share of the money — the link is the user's own and any transaction happens on that third-party service. The app itself displays only a QR code, never a payment method. The field is blank by default. If you would prefer we remove it, we will do so immediately and submit an updated build.

TERMINOLOGY, IN CASE IT CAUSED CONFUSION
"Personal" is one of three account roles chosen at sign-up (Student / Personal / Teacher) and means a solo learner practising without a teacher. It is not a paid plan. "Studio" on our website means a teaching studio — a teacher's group of students — not a subscription tier.

OUR INTENDED BUSINESS MODEL
Nothing is sold today. In future we intend two separate channels:

1. Institutional licences to schools and music departments, invoiced directly to the institution, as a classroom practice and management tool. We understand this falls under Guideline 3.1.3(c) Enterprise Services.

2. Optional individual subscriptions sold inside the app entirely through StoreKit In-App Purchase, with price and terms shown before purchase and a Restore Purchases option. This covers both an individual musician and a private teacher buying for their own studio — both are single-user consumer purchases, and both will use In-App Purchase, not our direct channel. We will not use the institutional channel to sell to individuals.

The app will contain no button, link or price pointing to any external purchase. School licensing is discussed only outside the app.

None of this exists in the build under review: no licences on sale, no institutional accounts, no seat or licence fields, no billing. We describe it only so our model is clear.
