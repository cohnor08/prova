Built for everyone in the music room

One app connects the practice triangle — the student who practices, the teacher who guides, and the parents and schools who make it possible.

FOR STUDENTS

A plan that fits you

  • AI-built daily practice plan for your level and goals
  • Guided practice player — one task at a time
  • Streaks, points and ranks that make practice stick
  • Tuner, metronome, chord & scale library built in
  • Ask Prova — an AI coach for any playing question
FOR TEACHERS

Your studio, organised

  • Connect students with a private join code
  • Assign tasks, songs and multi-week programs
  • Proof-of-practice videos from students
  • Lesson calendar with attendance and notes
  • Weekly practice reports emailed to parents — automatically
FOR SCHOOLS

Safe by design

  • No student-to-student private messaging — enforced on our servers, not just hidden in the app
  • Teachers see practice, parents see progress
  • Clear privacy policy, in-app account deletion
  • Organisation-level management for music departments
